FamilySearch

FamilySearch is run by a nonprofit and is genuinely free. A free account unlocks record search, the full-text search, and image access; the wiki and catalog do not even need an account.

FamilySearch Record Search

Free - account needed

billions of indexed historical records (births, marriages, deaths, census, military) from a nonprofit that never charges for records.

How: Create a free account, then search a name, place, and year range. Click 'View Record Images' when the index summary looks right. Sign in first or image links often dead-end.

Verified free on 2026-10-28

FamilySearch Full-Text Search

Free - account needed

AI-assisted keyword search across digitized deeds, probate packets, and county court papers that were never name-indexed.

How: Type a surname plus a place, filter by state. It reads handwriting, so try name spellings loosely. When it hits, read the whole page for the family context.

Verified free on 2026-10-28

FamilySearch Research Wiki

Free - no account

A free, county-by-county encyclopedia of what records exist, where they are kept, and what years survive for every U.S. county and country.

How: Search your ancestor's county. The wiki tells you which courthouse burned, which years survive, and often links the archive's own site. No account needed.

Verified free on 2026-10-28

FamilySearch Catalog

Free - no account

The library catalog of FamilySearch's microfilm and digitized county records, much of which never made it into regular record search.

How: Search a county name, choose 'United States, [State], [County] - Vital records' style listings, and look for camera icons meaning images are viewable at home.

Verified free on 2026-10-28

State & County Archives

National, state, and county-level archives and their volunteer-driven cousins. These hold the records the big sites have not digitized yet.

U.S. National Archives Genealogy Pages

Free - no account

The federal archive's own starting points for military pensions, immigration, land, and census records, many free online and the rest free by request.

How: Start with 'Resources for Genealogists,' then use the 'Order copies' route only for records you cannot reach free; NARA's held records are scanned into the Catalog at catalog.archives.gov.

Verified free on 2026-10-27

National Archives Catalog

Free - no account

NARA's full digitized holdings: every released federal census, WWII draft cards, naturalizations, and scanned pension files.

How: Search a soldier's name plus 'pension' or a place plus 'naturalization'. Records marked 'Archival' are yours to view and download free.

Verified free on 2026-10-27

Directory of State Archives (CoSA)

Free - no account

One linked list of every U.S. state's official archive, so you can jump straight to your state's vital-record and land-record holdings.

How: Open your state archive's genealogy or 'genealogists' page. Most states have free digital collections like will index scans and Confederate pension files.

Verified free on 2026-10-27

USGenWeb Project

Free - no account

Volunteer-run, free-text transcriptions and scanned records organized county by county for all 50 states.

How: Pick your state, then your county. Look for tombstone photos, old plat maps, and courthouse transcriptions from before paywall sites existed.

Verified free on 2026-10-27

The National Archives (UK) Research Guides

Free - no account

Hundreds of step-by-step guides and free record downloads for English, Welsh, and Scottish family history.

How: Choose a topic such as 'Tracing a will' or 'Royal Navy records.' Many records in the catalogue are digitized and free to view without reading-room visits.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

Census Lookups

Free routes into U.S. census and census-substitute records. The census releases are public domain, so fully free copies always exist somewhere.

1950 U.S. Census (NARA Portal)

Free - no account

The newest fully open U.S. census, released in 2022 with no 72-year wait and no paywall anywhere.

How: Search names directly from NARA's page, or browse an enumeration district map if a misspelling blocked a name match.

Verified free on 2026-10-27

Steve Morse One-Step Tools

Free - no account

Free search forms that slice census years and immigration indexes in ways the originals cannot, including 'city directory' and 'spellings-that-sound-like' options.

How: Pick 'US Census' and choose a year such as 1900. Limit to a county and a first letter of a name when the surname was mangled by an enumerator.

Verified free on 2026-10-27

Free Census Extraction & Transcription Forms

Free - no account

Printable blanks from NARA for transcribing each census year column by column, so nothing gets lost between screen and notebook.

How: Download the PDF for your census year, print, and hand-write findings as you read the images. Pair it with the binder-checklist page on this site.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

Free Newspaper Routes

Historic newspapers, free outright or free through your public library card. Newspapers are where obituaries, wedding notices, and small-town news live.

Chronicling America (Library of Congress)

Free - no account

Millions of fully searchable digitized U.S. newspaper pages from 1756-1963, all free straight from the Library of Congress.

How: Use 'Search Pages,' restrict to a state and date span, then try surname-only searching. OCR is imperfect, so also try nicknames and maiden names.

Verified free on 2026-10-28

U.S. Newspapers Directory (LoC)

Free - no account

A directory listing which titles were published in which county and what years survive on film, wherever they are held.

How: Search your county to learn whether the 1910 hometown paper even existed, and which library or archive holds surviving reels.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

Newspapers.com - Free Through Your Library Card

Free - library card

The huge commercial newspaper archive, free at home with a participating public library card (look for 'Library edition' in your library's databases list).

How: Log into your library's 'Research & Databases' page, find Newspapers.com Library Edition, and it should open with your card number. You pay nothing; always route through the library link.

Verified free on 2026-10-28

FultonSearch (Fulton History Mirror)

Free - no account

A free mirror of the famed Tom Fulton collection: millions of pages of New York State and other eastern newspapers.

How: Use the 'Search' box and keep terms short, one or two words. Try a town plus year range instead of a full family name.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

Google News Archive

Free - no account

Google's older digitized newspaper collection, still free, with full runs of many mid-sized city papers.

How: Browse by title or language; after opening a paper, use the search field inside that title rather than plain web search.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

Trove (National Library of Australia)

Free - no account

The world's most generous free newspaper archive, and a model route for Australian and British migrant families.

How: Search the Newspapers zone. Trove lets you correct OCR lines, so you can fix a mangled surname right in the record.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

NYS Historic Newspapers

Free - no account

New York's free state-wide archive of digitized small-town and big-city newspapers, fully searchable.

How: Search a surname, or browse titles by county. Great complement to FultonSearch when OCR results differ between the two.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

Cemeteries & Gravestones

Gravestone photos and burial indexes, plus the etiquette of using volunteer photo sites well.

Find A Grave

Free - account needed

Free community-built database of millions of memorials with photos, linked family members, and cemetery maps.

How: Search name plus state. Read the etiquette page on this site first: request photos politely, never rewrite other people's memorials, and thank the contributor.

Verified free on 2026-10-28

ANC Explorer (Arlington National Cemetery)

Free - no account

The cemetery's own free web map and app, with front-and-back headstone photos and plot locations for every Arlington burial.

How: Launch ANC Explorer in your browser from that page, then search last name plus first initial. No account, no fee.

Verified free on 2026-10-26

More Free Help

Volunteer lookups, mega-indexes, and free transcription tools to stretch your research further.

Cyndi's List

Free - no account

A hand-catalogued index of over 300,000 free genealogy links by place and topic, kept up by one volunteer for three decades.

How: Browse your county or ethnic-group category. It's the fastest way to find small county-level projects when a search engine only shows commercial sites.

Verified free on 2026-10-25

Random Acts of Genealogical Kindness

Free - no account

Volunteers who will take a gravestone photo or copy one document page in their hometown, free (you reimburse copying or travel costs only).

How: Search volunteers by state and county. Post one clear request with names, dates, and cemetery; be patient and say thank you.

Verified free on 2026-10-25

Digital Public Library of America

Free - no account

One search across photos, maps, and manuscripts from thousands of libraries, all publicly readable, plus family-research primary source sets.

How: Search an old family business name or street address; local-history scrapbooks and postcard collections often surface here that no other search reaches.

Verified free on 2026-10-25

Ancestor Hunt Free Obituary Links

Free - no account

A family-run free index pointing to county obituary boards, church records, and prison rosters around the U.S.

How: Open your state's listing and look for 'Obituary Insertion Indexes'; these often skip paywalls because the host sites are themselves free.

Verified free on 2026-10-25
